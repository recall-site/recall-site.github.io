Examples

RCL contracts to open in RECALL and experiment with. Copy the text, paste it into the editor on the Analysis tab and run the analysis.

1. A contract with a conflict

We declare that pay and cancel are mutually exclusive actions, but the contract obliges the buyer to perform both. 

conflict{
   global{(pay,cancel)};
};

{buyer,seller}O(pay);
{buyer,seller}O(cancel);

Analysis result:

[CONFLICT] A conflict was found in the analyzed contract.

Conflict found in state (s0)
Conflict: O(buyer,pay,seller) conflicts with [O(buyer,cancel,seller)]
Trace: (s0)

2. The Matrix: a dynamic contract

Morpheus offers Neo two choices: the red pill or the blue pill. His choice has consequences: he can save the world or simply wake up in his bed remembering nothing. If Neo fails to change the world, Smith will destroy the Matrix.

This example uses dynamic clauses ([redPill](...)), penalties (_/ ... /_), conjunction (^), concurrent actions (hideTrue&forget) and iteration ([1*]). 

/*************************************************************************
Morpheus offers to Neo two choices: Neo must choose between red or blue
pill. His choice have implications that will make him wake up and he
can save the World or will simply make him wake up in his bed and
not remembering nothing.
If Neo fails to change the World, Smith will destroy the Matrix.
**************************************************************************/
conflict{
   global{(showTrue,hideTrue), (redPill,bluePill)};
};

{neo,morpheus}[redPill](
   {morpheus,neo}O(showTrue)^
   {morpheus,neo}[showTrue](
      {neo}O(saveWorld)_/
         {smith}O(destroyMatrix)
      /_
   )
);
{neo,morpheus}[bluePill](
   {morpheus,neo}O(hideTrue&forget)^
   {morpheus,neo}[hideTrue](
      [1*]({neo}O(liveInIgnorance)) ^
      {smith}O(destroyMatrix)
   )
);

Analysis result:

[CONFLICT-FREE] The analyzed contract is conflict-free.
